01 / From scattered to settled

You know your life.
Your agent helps sort it.

tidymove is a set of instructions, forms, and local scripts. It gives your AI agent a careful process to follow, with you making the decisions that matter.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Find it. Talk it through.

List what is there. Compare old and current copies. Answer a few clear questions whenever a folder's purpose is uncertain.

02 — MAKE A PLAN

A home that makes sense.

Choose a useful layout. See what will be copied, how much space it needs, and what stays untouched. Review before running.

03 — COPY & CHECK

Bring it over. Keep the original.

Copy approved files, compare their contents, and try them yourself. Finish with a record of what worked and what's left.

02 / A run, illustrated

Less guessing.
Better questions.

You should not need to understand a strange filename to make a good decision. Your agent explains the context, recommends an option, and waits for your answer.

03 / Care comes first

A fresh start.
With a way back.

  • ✓

    Originals stay put.

    No deleting, moving, or repairing original files. Protect the source against writes before copying. Keep it for recovery.

  • ✓

    Preview first. Verify after.

    Scripts start with a preview. By default, every selected file gets a content fingerprint check. Conflicting copies stop the run.

  • ✓

    Decisions stay with you.

    You approve uploads, sign-ins, system changes, and switching to new copies. Passwords and keys are handled by you, privately.

  • ✓

    Unknowns stay visible.

    Skipped files and failed checks stay in the record. A sample check is labeled as a sample. A finished copy is not a tested backup.

These safeguards cannot guarantee against mistakes or hardware failure. Filename checks cannot find every secret. Your AI provider may receive conversation and tool context; keep sensitive records private.

05 / Before you begin

A few good
questions.

Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Start by describing your files and devices. Your agent should explain each step. Some disk-protection and app-recovery steps need technical help; stop when you cannot confirm what a command will affect.

Is this an app I download?

It's a kit for an AI agent with access to local files: a Claude Code plugin and a plain skill folder for other tools. It does not come with an AI account or a desktop app.

Will it delete or upload my files?

The bundled scripts copy locally and retain originals, conflicts, and extra destination files. Any upload is a separate operation you approve. Your AI tool may still send prompts and tool results to its provider.

Which computers does it support?

Windows PowerShell 5.1 is the tested script path. macOS and Linux recipes are experimental and untested on those platforms. The general planning process can still help you work through what to keep.

Where did the process come from?

The author reports a real migration spread across nine days, with questions, pauses, and repeated checks. The private records are not published, and this is not a promise about your migration's duration. Read the generalized story.

What if something fails?

Stop that batch and keep the original, partial copy, and logs. Review the failure before trying again. Never disable a safety check to finish faster. Keep an independent backup on another device or an approved service.
